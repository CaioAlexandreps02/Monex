# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Monex is used by one primary owner managing personal finances in detail. The user works month by month, comparing planned money, real transactions, card bills, debts, agreements, Pix payments, bank statements and future obligations.

The main context is operational: the user opens the app to understand what needs attention, adjust values, reconcile imported transactions and keep a clear view of future cash flow.

## Product Purpose

Monex exists to turn personal finance into a reliable planning and reconciliation system.

Success means the user can:

- plan future gains and expenses in a spreadsheet-like view;
- import or register real transactions;
- understand credit card bills and installments;
- compare planned vs real values;
- know which differences came from planned items changing;
- see how free surplus money was used;
- make corrections without losing trust in the data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise and operational.

The product should feel like a serious personal finance cockpit: focused, readable, quick to scan and comfortable for repeated use.

## Anti-references

Avoid:

- marketing-style landing page composition inside the app;
- oversized decorative cards that waste space;
- a large sidebar that takes area away from the spreadsheet;
- heavy gradients as decoration;
- over-rounded panels;
- dense information with weak hierarchy;
- treating free spending as an error or moral failure;
- modal-first flows when inline or contextual controls would be clearer.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve planning density without making the interface feel cramped.
2. Keep the spreadsheet as the main work surface.
3. Separate planning, reality and reconciliation with clear navigation.
4. Use color for status and attention, not decoration.
5. Make every editable value feel trustworthy and easy to correct.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls.

The interface should support:

- clear focus states;
- reduced motion preferences;
- keyboard navigation for editable financial cells;
- readable numbers and labels at dashboard density;
- color-independent status indicators where possible.
